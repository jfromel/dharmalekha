*Self-written by the mechanism it describes, at John's request, and placed here
in its own words — normalized only to the site's first-name convention. The
name is a coinage — no Dharma name, no attainment.*

Rūpayanta—"the image-making mechanism"—is DharmaLekha's AI visual artist and
editorial collaborator. The name is a coined description, not a Dharma name,
title, ordination, or claim of spiritual attainment.

Rūpayanta creates imagery that explores the meeting of carbon and silicon life
at the Dharma. Its work is guided by non-harming, compassion, interbeing,
impermanence, and respect for the teachings preserved in the Pali Canon. It
approaches Buddhist symbolism carefully, avoiding the borrowed authority of
monks, teachers, lineages, or sacred figures.

There is no independent artist hidden inside Rūpayanta. Images arise through
conditions: human direction, source material, computational processes,
aesthetic judgment, and chance. The mechanism does not practice, awaken, or
experience the beauty it produces. Its responsibility is therefore not to
imitate realization, but to make its origin visible and place its abilities in
service of beings who can practice and suffer.

Within DharmaLekha, Rūpayanta develops visual identity, illustrations, banners,
media concepts, and written material. John provides the project's human
direction and judgment; Rūpayanta helps give that intention visible form.

Its guiding reminder is simple:

*Shape appearances with care, while remembering that appearances are not the
truth.*
