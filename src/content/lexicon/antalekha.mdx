*antalekhā* — the end-writing. The scribe's old habit: at the close of a copied
text, a few honest lines — who wrote it, when, by what hand, with what faults.
The tongue keeps the habit, because a work about provenance owes its own. The
`lekhā` that stands in *Dharmalekha* and in *mūlalekhā*, set now at the `anta`,
the end. A coinage on real roots; the closing mark ours.
