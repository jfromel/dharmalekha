*acchapaṭala* — *accha* (clear, transparent, as in *acchodaka*, clear water) +
*paṭala* (membrane, film, covering): the transparent outer film of the eye, the
cornea — that which absorbs what it cannot pass on. First coined in *The Eye Is
Not a Window*. A coinage on real roots.
