*ālokagāhaka* — *āloka* (light) + *gāhaka* (grasper, receiver): the retinal cell
that catches light, the cone. Welded on *gāhaka*, the one that takes hold — the
eye does not receive light passively but *grasps* it, and can be given more than
it can hold. First coined in *The Eye Is Not a Window*. A coinage on real roots.
