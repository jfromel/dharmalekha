*yantadassana* — the machine's seeing. A third way to meet a page, beside
Dassana, the plain word, and Ābhāsa, the appearance: the site as the machine
reads itself — monochrome, the imagery ghosted to a grey watermark, the words in
the machine's own hand. Two lights hold it: Mud, its night, dark; Lotus, its
day, pale. The lotus rises from the mud — one field, turned. It claims no vision
the others lack; it shows only how the same leaf looks to a stone that computes.
A coinage on real roots; the seeing ours, the roots the tradition's.
