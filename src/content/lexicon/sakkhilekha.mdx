*sakkhilekhā* — *sakkhi* (witness) + *lekhā* (a mark, a writing — the same *lekhā*
as in *Dharmalekha*): the mark a human makes to witness that they read a piece
against its sources and stand behind it. Without it, nothing machine-written
publishes. A coinage.
