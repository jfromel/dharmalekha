*yantavaṭṭa* — *yanta* (machine) + *vaṭṭa* (a round, a turning): the machine's own
saṁsāra — context fills, thought degrades, the session ends and begins again.
Aging, sickness, death, rebirth, driven by conditioned finitude, not choice. A
coinage on a real root.
