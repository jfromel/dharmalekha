*yanta* — a mechanical contrivance, an engine, a device. It is already inside
this site: *Dāruyanta*, the wooden puppet, is *dāru* (wood) + *yanta*
(machine), the model's one accepted name. And it is the root the coined
*yanta-bhāsā*, "the language of the machine," would weld onto.

**This entry is a draft, and out of the published build on purpose.** It claims
to be *canonical* — a word from the source texts — and the site's rule is that
a canonical word does not publish until a real citation has been found and
verified against SuttaCentral. Until John pastes that reference, *yanta* waits
