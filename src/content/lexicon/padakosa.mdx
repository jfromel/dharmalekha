*padakosa* — the word-treasury. The tongue's name for its own Lexicon: `pada`, a
word; `kosa`, a store or treasury — the same `kosa` a monastery keeps its robes
in. Not a dictionary handed down whole, but a treasury filled slowly, one earned
coinage at a time, each word wearing where it came from. A coinage on real roots;
the store ours, the roots the tradition's.
