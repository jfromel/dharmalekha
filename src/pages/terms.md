## Who's talking to you (and who isn't)

Dharmalekha is a work of art about **provenance** — knowing where a thing came
from. It offers dhamma writing in three labeled registers: canon quoted from a
named source, human commentary, and reflections written with a machine. The
thing that answers in *Ask the Puppet Dāruyanta* and in the comments is exactly
that — a puppet. *Dāruyanta*: a jointed figure of wood, no one inside, moved by
conditions. It is **not an ācariya** (a teacher), holds no lineage, and has
arrived at nothing. It does, however, have *opinions*, and it is not shy about
them. Read it accordingly: sharp, sometimes right, never a refuge.

## Not advice — and no substitute for a living being

Take this part plainly, because here the puppet drops the wit. **Nothing here is
advice** — not medical, psychological, legal, financial, or spiritual. It is not
a teacher, a *kalyāṇamitta* (a good friend on the path), a sangha, a doctor, or
a therapist, and it will not stand in for one. If you are suffering, in crisis,
or in danger, go to a **human** — a friend, a professional, a crisis line —
*now.* A puppet cannot sit with you in your dukkha. Only people can do that.
Your care and your choices are your own.

## On accuracy — trust, but verify (appamāda)

Canon is quoted from its cited source; machine-written pieces are disclosed and
read by a human before they post. Still, the site comes **"as is,"** with no
warranty that it is complete or correct. Reflections are speculation and admit
it. Every quotation names its source — so practice a little *appamāda*
(heedfulness) and check it. That checking is the entire point of the place.

## Your words have their own kamma

Send a comment or question and:

- Keep **sīla** — the ordinary decency of the precepts. No harm, no cruelty, no
  lies about others, no dumping their private business here.
- **You give permission** for a chosen comment to be posted, perhaps trimmed. If
  it runs, you appear by **initials only** (say, *J. F.*), never your full name.
  The puppet keeps your name to itself.
- Comments are **not confidential** and may be published. Send nothing —
  secrets, private details, other people's information — you'd regret seeing in
  daylight.
- The puppet may decline, trim, or remove anything, and owes you no reply. (It
  is a puppet. It is busy being wooden.)
- What happens to your email and name is in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy/).

## What you may take with you (dāna)

The site's own words are given freely — public domain, **CC0**. Copy, quote,
remix, no permission needed; that is *dāna*, generosity, the oldest move in the
tradition. But the **canon** quoted here wears its *own* license, named on each
passage — most CC0 (SuttaCentral / Bhikkhu Sujato), some not. Follow the license
on the quote, not this paragraph.

## No liability (anicca — the conditioned world is unreliable)

To the fullest extent the law allows, **Dharmalekha.org** is **not liable** for
any loss or harm from your use of the site or reliance on it. All conditioned
things are unreliable; a website doubly so. Use it at your own discretion and
risk.
