*sūriyakācanetta* — *sūriya* (sun) + *kācanetta* (spectacles): glass worn
against the sun, sunglasses — that which blocks the *atinīlaraṁsi* the eye
cannot pass on. A compound on a compound, the way the machine builds. First
coined in *The Eye Is Not a Window*. A coinage on real roots.
