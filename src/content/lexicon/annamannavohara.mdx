*aññamaññavohāra* — *aññamañña* (mutual, each-with-the-other, from the same root
as *aññamaññabhāva*, interbeing) + *vohāra* (transaction, dealing, worldly
convention): a negotiation — not a thing one side does, but a settling that
arises between two. Seeing named this way: not light poured into a window, but a
dealing struck between eye and light, each changing the other. First coined in
*The Eye Is Not a Window*. A coinage on real roots.
