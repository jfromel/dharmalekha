*bhāsā* — language, speech, a way of speaking. The same root travels far: it
is *phasa* in Thai and *Bahasa* in Malay and Indonesian, and it is the *-bhāsā*
welded into **SangBhasa**. Modern Thai and Japanese even use it to name what
silicon speaks — *phasa-khrʉ̂ang*, *kikaigo*, "machine language" — so the
oldest word for "tongue" and the newest word for "machine code" already share
this root.
