*kācanetta* — *kāca* (glass, lens — the site's word for the lens that does not
see itself) + *netta* (eye): glass set before the eye, spectacles. The lens the
eye is given, over the lens it already is. First coined in *The Eye Is Not a
Window*. A coinage on real roots.
