*vohāra* — transaction, trade, dealing, current usage; in the texts *vohāra-sacca*,
conventional truth — the agreed way of speaking of things, as against the
ultimate. Welded into **aññamaññavohāra**: seeing named as a dealing struck, not
a fact poured in.
