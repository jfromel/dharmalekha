*Last updated: 2026-07-26.*

**The vow, up front:** what you hand this site is used only for what you asked —
plus, honestly, to tell you about our *own* goods if you opt in (see below) —
and it is **never sold, never traded, and never given to any advertiser,
organization, person, or corporation. Ever.** Not marketing language. Treat it
as a precept.

## What the site gathers (very little)

- **Only what you offer.** A comment or question is text; a name and email are
  *optional*, only if you want a reply. Subscribing ("Stay on the Path") means an
  email and a cadence. No logins, no profiles, no hidden fields.
- **What it refuses to gather:** **no tracking cookies**, no advertising profile,
  no following you across the web. Any analytics are **cookieless and aggregate**
  — how many read a thing, nothing that names you. If analytics are ever added,
  they will be cookieless and privacy-respecting (a tool such as Plausible or
  Fathom).

## What it's used for — including, honestly, selling you things

- Your **name and email** are used to *reply to you*, and — if you chose "Stay on
  the Path" — to send new pieces at your chosen cadence.
- **The honest part:** Dharmalekha.org intends to open a small shop of goods one
  day, at **dharmalekha.com** — a separate house from this one. There will be a
  separate box, **"Keep me updated on new products at dharmalekha.com,"** and
  *only* if you tick it will we email you about our own goods. The Dhamma
  subscription never signs you up for sales; the two lists are kept apart.
  First-party only.
- We will **never** sell, rent, trade, or hand your information to any
  advertiser, organization, person, or corporation — not for money, not for
  favor, not ever. Your data stays in the puppet's keeping.
- A published **comment** shows your **initials only** (e.g., *J. F.*). Your
  **email is never published** — it is only how you're reached.

## Where it lives (never in the open)

- Submissions pass through **Formspree** to the keeper's own inbox; subscriptions
  (when they open) will be held by a **reputable newsletter service**, which
  stores your email and cadence and handles unsubscribing.
- **The site's code is public and open-source — and holds none of your data.**
  Your information is *never* committed to that public repository, where it could
  be exposed. It lives only with the services above and on the keeper's own
  machine. By design, and not up for negotiation.

## Your choices (the door is never locked)

- **Unsubscribe** whenever — every email carries the link; it takes effect at
  once.
- **See, correct, or delete** anything held about you — write to
  **contact@dharmalekha.org**, and it is done.
- Read the whole site **giving nothing.** Your data is the price of a *reply* or
  a *subscription*, never of reading.

## How long (no hoarding)

Only as long as needed — a subscriber's email until they leave; a question's
contact until it's answered or removal is asked. Nothing kept "just in case."
Non-attachment, applied to your inbox.

## Children

Not for children. Nothing is knowingly gathered from anyone **under 16**. If a
child has sent something, tell **contact@dharmalekha.org** and it will be
deleted.

## Security, without the theater

Your data is guarded with reasonable care, kept out of the public repo, and
minimized so there is little to lose. No system is perfectly secure, and this
policy will not pretend otherwise. The best lock is the one already fitted: the
site collects almost nothing.

## This policy is anicca

If the practices change, so does this, dated here — and the golden rule holds:
**the words must always match what the site actually does.** If they ever drift,
the behavior is the truth and the words are the bug. Tell
**contact@dharmalekha.org** and it gets fixed.

---

*Closing: a place that asks for your trust has one job — to deserve it. So it
takes little, says plainly what it takes, keeps its word on the rest, and hands
the Dhamma over as dāna, free. The puppet will argue with you about almost
anything. It will not lie to you about this.*
