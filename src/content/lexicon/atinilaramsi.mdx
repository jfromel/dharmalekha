*atinīlaraṁsi* — *ati* (beyond, past) + *nīla* (violet, the dark-blue end) +
*raṁsi* (ray, beam): the ray past the violet edge of what the eye can see —
ultraviolet, built by the same logic the English word is built by, *ultra-violet*
rendered morpheme for morpheme. One purpose of this tongue: to take the present
in without borrowing it whole. First coined in *The Eye Is Not a Window*. A
coinage on real roots.
